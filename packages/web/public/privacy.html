Privacy Policy & Wellness Safety

Breath in Flow stores your practice and pulse data on your device.

Data on your device

Breath in Flow does not collect, sell, or automatically transmit personal or wellness data. It has no accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or ads. Sessions, presets, preferences, and any saved pulse readings or assessments stay in localStorage and IndexedDB until you erase them.

Optional camera and motion access

Pulse Variability checks can use your camera and torch to estimate pulse, or motion sensors to estimate heartbeat timing. The app processes these signals in real time on your device. It does not record, store, or transmit photos or video. You can decline or revoke permission and still use guided breathing.

Backups and support

Export backup in Help & Guide → Data creates a copy of your local data for you to keep or share. The file can contain personal information. You decide where to save it and whom to share it with.

If you email support, we use your address and the information you choose to send to respond. The in-app feedback action can include app and device details. It does not attach practice data, pulse readings, or sensor recordings.

Erase your data

Go to Help & Guide → Data, choose Erase all local data, and confirm. This permanently removes your sessions, presets, pulse readings, assessments, and saved preferences from the app on this device. Delete any copies you exported separately wherever you saved them.

General wellness and breath-hold safety

Breath in Flow is for general wellness and relaxation. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Never practice extended breath holds in water, while driving, or if you feel dizzy or lightheaded.

Children’s privacy and contact

Breath in Flow is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

For privacy or support questions, email hello@breathinflow.app.
