# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Turn the metronome and voice guidance on or off independently during Practice. Recommended routines use your current audio settings.
- Stop pauses the timer and cues while you decide whether to continue, save your progress, or exit.
- Metronome clicks are louder in the Woodblock, Water, Wind, and Chimes themes. The beat stays even through fractional phases, and a separate tone marks each phase change.
- Bundled voice cues skip leading silence and fit one-second breathing phases.
- Practice announces phase changes to screen readers. Sensor trials show the current phase, and the assessment setup offers **Test audio** before you position your phone.
